About Us

Coaching that starts with your body, not a template

Fitgurukannada is a Bengaluru-based fitness coaching service specialising in fat loss, body recomposition, and lean muscle gain. Every client starts the same way: your real stats, lifestyle, and a current photo — so your coach builds a plan around where you actually are.

No crash diets, no "lose 10kg in 10 days" promises. Realistic plans, checked in on, and adjusted as you progress.
